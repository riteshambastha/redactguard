Self-hosted video PII redaction

Redact faces, on-screen text, and spoken PII from video - without it ever leaving your infrastructure.

Why it's built this way

Redaction you can actually verify

Every design decision here is documented as an ADR in the repository - this isn't marketing copy standing in for an architecture no one can see.

🔒

Self-hosted, always

Runs on your own infrastructure via Docker or a local Python install. Detection models run locally - the only network call this project ever makes is an optional, one-time model download if you enable spoken-PII detection.

🧩

Ensemble detection, not one model

Every PII type is checked by two independent, differently-failing detectors and combined by agreement voting - a single model's blind spot doesn't become your blind spot.

🔄

Verify-then-retry, never a silent miss

After redacting, RedactGuard re-scans its own output to confirm nothing was missed, escalating and retrying automatically. If it still can't resolve everything, it says so - it never silently ships a gap.

📋

Policy-as-code

Redaction rules are versioned YAML profiles, not a hidden confidence threshold - review, diff, and check them into your own compliance documentation.

The pipeline

Detect, redact, verify, retry

1

Detect

Sampled frames and audio run through every enabled detector independently.

2

Vote

Detections are combined by agreement threshold - two independent hits, not one guess.

3

Redact

Trusted spans are blurred or muted at the source's native frame rate.

4

Verify

The redacted draft is re-scanned; anything still flagged triggers an escalated retry.
